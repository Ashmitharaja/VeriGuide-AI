VFD-7 VARIABLE FREQUENCY DRIVE FAULT CODE REFERENCE

FAULT F01 OVERCURRENT
F01 is shown when the output current exceeds 200 percent of the drive rated current. Causes are a short circuit in the motor cable, an acceleration time that is too short, or a jammed load. Increase the acceleration time parameter P1.10 and check the motor cable insulation.

FAULT F02 OVERVOLTAGE
F02 is shown when the DC bus voltage exceeds 820 V. It usually happens during fast deceleration of a high-inertia load. Increase the deceleration time parameter P1.11 or fit a braking resistor.

FAULT F04 UNDERVOLTAGE
F04 is shown when the DC bus voltage falls below 400 V. Check the incoming supply voltage and the input fuses.

FAULT F07 DRIVE OVERTEMPERATURE
F07 means the heatsink temperature of the drive has exceeded 90 °C. Check that the drive cooling fan is running, clean the heatsink, and make sure the cabinet ambient temperature is below 40 °C. The fault can be reset only after the heatsink cools below 70 °C.

FAULT F09 MOTOR OVERLOAD
F09 means the electronic motor thermal model has reached 100 percent. Check the motor current against the value in parameter P2.01 (motor rated current) and reduce the mechanical load.

FAULT F12 INPUT PHASE LOSS
F12 means one of the three input phases is missing. Check the input fuses and supply cable connections.

RESETTING FAULTS
Press the STOP/RESET key on the keypad to reset a fault once its cause has been removed. Three resets of the same fault within 10 minutes lock the drive, and it must be power cycled.
