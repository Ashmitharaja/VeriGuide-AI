# Induction Motor Maintenance Manual (Model IM-300)

## Overview
The IM-300 is a three-phase totally enclosed fan-cooled (TEFC) induction motor rated for 400 V, 50 Hz operation. Rated output is 15 kW at 1460 rpm. The insulation is Class F with a Class B temperature rise.

## Motor Overheating
Overheating is the most common cause of premature insulation failure. The normal frame temperature at full load is up to 80 °C. A frame temperature above 95 °C indicates a fault that must be investigated.
Common causes of overheating are: overload beyond the rated current, blocked or dirty cooling fins, a damaged or missing cooling fan, high ambient temperature above 40 °C, supply voltage unbalance greater than 2 percent, single phasing (loss of one supply phase), and too many starts per hour.
To diagnose overheating, first measure the running current in all three phases with a clamp meter and compare it with the nameplate full load current of 28 A. Then check the supply voltage at the motor terminals for unbalance. Clean the cooling fins and inspect the fan cover for blockages. Verify that the fan is fitted and rotating.
The IM-300 must not be started more than 6 times per hour. Frequent starting causes the rotor and stator windings to overheat.

## Abnormal Noise and Vibration
A humming noise with no rotation usually indicates single phasing. Grinding or rumbling noise usually indicates worn bearings. Vibration above 4.5 mm/s RMS requires checking shaft alignment, foundation bolts and rotor balance.

## Bearing Lubrication
The drive-end and non-drive-end bearings are 6309-2Z type. Regrease the bearings every 4000 operating hours using lithium-complex grease, 15 grams per bearing. Over-greasing also causes bearing overheating.

## Insulation Resistance Test
Measure insulation resistance with a 500 V megger between each winding and the frame. The minimum acceptable value is 1 megaohm. Values below 1 megaohm indicate moisture or contaminated windings; dry the motor at 80 °C before re-testing.

## Motor Does Not Start
If the motor does not start, check that the supply isolator is closed, the overload relay is reset, and all three fuses are healthy. Check the control circuit for an open emergency stop.
