# Centrifugal Pump Troubleshooting Guide (CP-50 Series)

## Pump Runs But No Flow
If the pump runs but delivers no flow, the most likely cause is that the pump is not primed. Fill the casing and suction line with liquid before starting. Other causes are a closed suction or discharge valve, a clogged suction strainer, an air leak in the suction line, or wrong direction of rotation.
Check the direction of rotation against the arrow on the pump casing. If it is reversed, swap any two of the three motor supply phases.

## Low Discharge Pressure
Low discharge pressure is caused by a worn impeller, excessive wear ring clearance, speed lower than rated, or air entrainment. The wear ring clearance must not exceed 0.6 mm; replace the wear rings if it does.

## Cavitation
Cavitation produces a noise like gravel passing through the pump and causes pitting damage on the impeller. It occurs when the available NPSH is lower than the required NPSH of 3.5 m. To reduce cavitation, lower the suction lift, increase the suction pipe diameter, clean the suction strainer, or reduce the liquid temperature.

## Mechanical Seal Leakage
A small leakage of up to 5 drops per minute is acceptable during the first hour of running-in. Continuous leakage after running-in means the seal faces are damaged and the mechanical seal must be replaced. Never run the pump dry, because dry running destroys the seal faces within seconds.

## Pump Overheating
The pump casing overheats when it runs against a closed discharge valve for a long time. Do not operate the CP-50 against a closed discharge valve for more than 2 minutes.
